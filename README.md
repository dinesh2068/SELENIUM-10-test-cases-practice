# SELENIUM AUTOMATION – 10 TEST CASES

## Test Cases

### T1 – Open Online Shopping Website
**Testcase:** Customer opens the online shopping website.
**Output:** Shopping website opens successfully.

### T2 – Alert Accept
**Testcase:** Customer clicks Delete/Remove Product and the confirmation popup appears.
**Output:** Product deletion is confirmed.

### T3 – Alert Dismiss
**Testcase:** Customer clicks Delete/Remove Product but chooses Cancel.
**Output:** Product remains in the cart.

### T4 – Prompt Alert
**Testcase:** Customer enters a name/coupon/customer information in a prompt popup.
**Output:** Entered information is submitted successfully.

### T5 – Mouse Hover
**Testcase:** Customer moves the mouse over the Products/Category menu.
**Output:** Product categories/submenu are displayed.

### T6 – Double Click
**Testcase:** Customer double-clicks a product/button.
**Output:** Double-click action is performed successfully.

### T7 – Drag and Drop
**Testcase:** Customer drags a product/item into a shopping cart area.
**Output:** Product is moved to the target area.

### T8 – Explicit Wait
**Testcase:** Customer searches for a product and waits for the product results to load.
**Output:** Product is displayed successfully.

### T9 – Clickable Wait
**Testcase:** Customer completes checkout and waits until the Place Order/Finish button becomes clickable.
**Output:** Order is submitted successfully.

### T10 – Alert Wait
**Testcase:** Customer completes the purchase and waits for the order confirmation popup.
**Output:** Confirmation alert is handled successfully.
